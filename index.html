SUPE PINES

a neighborhood superhero story, told together, one shift at a time
✦ ⁘ ✦

Something's wrong in Millhaven. Around this table — or this single glowing screen — you'll play the neighborhood's own masked defenders, chase down the Threat behind it, and write the whole case up for the record.

A superhero re-imagining after the design of Tall Pines by Miles Gaborit, by way of its sibling Bleakwood Vale.
All card text herein is original. For 1–6 storytellers, about half an hour each.
New to Supe Pines? Read “How the Case Is Run” first — about three minutes. A full case runs roughly 20–40 minutes per storyteller. ✕
A word before we begin: this game involves violence, vigilantism, and personal trauma played for real stakes — with plenty of room for comedy too. Agree as a group that anyone may strike any element from the story at any time — no questions asked, no reasons owed. The stricken thing simply never was.
